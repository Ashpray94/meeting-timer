Never miss a meeting again.

Meeting Timer shows a countdown right before each meeting on your Google Calendar, and gets more insistent until you join, snooze or dismiss it.

How it works

  • Sign in with Google once. Meeting Timer reads your upcoming meetings.
  • Five minutes before a meeting, a countdown appears on your screen with one-click Join.
  • Join, snooze or dismiss on one device and your other devices follow.

Available for

Windows 10 and 11, and Android 8 or later.
